# Must-cover presets

Offer the matching preset at gate step G4, then let the teacher edit it. Every preset also includes: **the lesson's own story/concept/process** and **author/source details** (when there is an author).

## Hindi (second/first language) — from an AP Class 10 Hindi teacher
Objective bits on: तत्सम–तद्भव · शब्दार्थ · समास · संधि-विच्छेद · अंक (numbers ↔ words, e.g. 1977 → उन्नीस सौ सतहत्तर) · कारक · उल्टे शब्द (विलोम) · पर्याय शब्द (पर्यायवाची) · शुद्ध शब्द (spelling) · कवि/लेखक परिचय.
Plus: the lesson's story/poem meaning and message in short and long answers.

## Telugu / other Indian languages
Synonyms (పర్యాయపదాలు), opposites (వ్యతిరేక పదాలు), word meanings (అర్థాలు), సంధులు, సమాసాలు, ప్రకృతి–వికృతి, అలంకారాలు, ఛందస్సు (if taught), spelling, author intro, lesson meaning.

## English (language)
Vocabulary (meanings, synonyms, antonyms), parts of speech, tenses, voice, reported speech, articles/prepositions, spelling, punctuation, author intro, comprehension of the lesson, short and long answers on theme/characters.

## Mathematics
Definitions and terms, formulas, one item per worked-example type in the chapter, word problems, units/conversions, diagrams/graphs if in the chapter, "find the mistake", one higher-order (thinking) item within syllabus.

## Science
Definitions, facts and terms, labelled diagrams, processes/steps, differences between, examples, activities/experiments from the chapter, units, numerical problems (Physics/Chemistry), reasons ("give reasons"), real-life application.

## Social studies
Key terms, dates and events, people and places, causes and effects, map work, differences between, timelines, source/picture reading, short notes, application to today.
