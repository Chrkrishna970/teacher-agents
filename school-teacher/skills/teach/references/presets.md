# Must-cover presets

Offer the matching preset at gate step G4, then let the teacher edit it. Every preset also includes: **the lesson's own story/concept/process** and **author/source details** (when there is an author). Name each element in the teacher's own terms (the board's words, in the paper's language). If no preset fits, build the list with the teacher from the lesson's exercises and `concepts.md`.

## Any language subject (first, second or third language) — generic
Word meanings · synonyms · antonyms · spelling (correct word) · word formation (prefix/suffix/compounds) · parts of speech · gender / number / case or tense (as the language has) · sentence correction / ordering · idioms & proverbs from the lesson · numbers in words · figures of speech · comprehension of the lesson · author/poet introduction · lesson meaning and message (short + long answers) · letter/essay/creative writing if the board tests it.

तत्सम–तद्भव · शब्दार्थ · समास · संधि-विच्छेद · अंक (numbers ↔ words, e.g. 1977 → उन्नीस सौ सतहत्तर) · कारक · उल्टे शब्द (विलोम) · पर्याय शब्द (पर्यायवाची) · शुद्ध शब्द · कवि/लेखक परिचय · lesson meaning and message.

### English (language)
Vocabulary (meanings, synonyms, antonyms) · parts of speech · tenses · voice · reported speech · articles / prepositions / determiners · spelling · punctuation · phrasal verbs / idioms from the lesson · author intro · comprehension · theme / characters (short + long) · letter / paragraph writing if tested.

### Other foreign languages (French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese…)
Vocabulary · articles & gender · verb conjugation / tenses · agreement · word order · question forms · numbers & dates · culture notes from the lesson · reading comprehension · short writing task. For CJK: characters/kanji reading & writing, measure words / particles, stroke order if taught.

## Mathematics
Definitions & terms · formulas · one item per worked-example type in the chapter · word problems · units & conversions · diagrams / constructions / graphs (if in the chapter) · "find the mistake" · estimation / mental maths · one higher-order (thinking) item within the syllabus. Written answers need working-space boxes.

## Science (General / EVS)
Definitions · facts & terms · labelled diagrams · processes / steps · differences between · examples · activities / experiments from the chapter · units · give reasons · real-life application · safety.
- **Physics:** + formulas, numericals (formula, working, answer with unit), graphs, ray diagrams/circuits.
- **Chemistry:** + equations (balance), symbols & valency, properties, lab tests, numericals (mole etc. as taught).
- **Biology:** + labelled diagrams, life processes, classification, functions of parts, flow charts.

## Social studies
- **History:** dates & events · people & places · causes & effects · timelines · sources/pictures.
- **Geography:** terms · map work (locate & label) · processes · resources · climate data / graphs.
- **Civics / Political science:** terms · rights & duties · institutions & functions · case studies.
- **Economics:** terms · data / tables / graphs · examples from daily life · differences between.

## Computer science / IT
Terms & definitions · parts & functions · shortcut keys / commands · output of a program · find & fix the error · write a short program / algorithm / flowchart · practical steps.

## Commerce / Accountancy / Business
Terms · principles · journal entries / ledgers / statements (as taught) · numericals with formats · differences between · case-based questions.

## Arts, music, physical education, moral/value education
Terms · people & works · techniques / rules / steps · identification from pictures · short reflective answers.
